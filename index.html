SAFETY FIRST

Simple habits that matter

A few basic habits can help you respond more safely.

🧘

Stay calm

Pause, assess the situation and avoid unnecessary panic.

🧼

Keep things clean

Use clean hands and clean dressings whenever possible.

📞

Know when to call

Serious or worsening situations need professional medical help.

🩹

Protect the injury

Stop activities that could make an injury worse.

👀

Monitor symptoms

Watch for symptoms that become severe or change over time.

👨‍⚕️

Get medical advice

First-aid information does not replace professional medical care.
